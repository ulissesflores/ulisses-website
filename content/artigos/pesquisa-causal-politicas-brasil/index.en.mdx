Of the 27 economic policy decisions I gathered since the Real Plan (the stabilization plan that created the real currency), the filter of scientific journals found no study at all for 10. For another 10 it found studies, but none measures the policy. Only 7 have one that does, and in just 4 it measures without caveat: Bolsa Família (cash transfers), Minha Casa Minha Vida (housing), Auxílio Emergencial (pandemic cash aid) and deforestation.

That filter catches journals in the top quartile (Q1) of the SJR (*SCImago Journal Rank*, a journal ranking). I went to check what lies outside it: working papers from institutions such as the Central Bank add little, and the exception without caveat is Lava Jato (Car Wash), with a study that measured a drop in employment at the investigated firms. Zero in this filter is not zero in the literature.

Federal government cards and travel, in the terms with data, come to between 0.0661% and 0.1474% of primary spending, which is everything the Union (Brazil's federal government) spends in a year, apart from interest: INSS (social security) benefits, payroll, health, education, transfers to states and municipalities, investment. Primary spending is not travel spending. None of this says who governed better. I took up that other question in [which government was Brazil's best](/artigos/raio-x-do-brasil-por-governo).

## Without the second dish, what you see is the taste, not the effect of the salt

Two dishes of rice, the same pot, the same fire. One with salt. The other with none. Tasting only the first tells you the rice is salty. It does not tell you what the salt did. The second dish is what would have happened. The effect of the policy is the difference from the path not taken.

## The four levels are not equivalent, and describing the dish is not measuring the salt

The scale was written before the reading. What counts is what the abstract describes, not the isolated word. Level 1, identification by design: the rule allocates who received the policy. Level 2, observation with adjustment: nobody drew lots; similar cases are compared. Level 3: a model simulates the dish nobody saw. Level 4: the dish served is described. I is indeterminate.

Level 1. Rural credit and deforestation, 2019, in part, improves. *"Difference-in-differences estimations based on a panel of municipalities show that the policy change led to a substantial reduction in deforestation, mostly in municipalities where cattle ranching is the leading economic activity."* Difference-in-differences compares the change among those who were hit with the change among those who were not. A panel is the same municipality over time.

Level 2. Conservation and prices, 2015, in part, improves. *"After controlling for price effects, we find that conservation policies implemented beginning in 2004 and 2008 significantly contributed to the curbing of deforestation."*

Level 3. Pensions, 2025, in part, improves. *"Calibrated to Brazil, the model shows that unifying pension systems and raising the minimum retirement age reduce the pension deficit by nearly 40 percent, while increasing output, capital accumulation, and welfare, despite redistributive effects across age groups, sectors, and along the transition path."* To calibrate is to fit the model to the country's numbers. The nearly 40 percent is the model's own sentence.

Level 4. Indigenous lands and mining, 2025, in part, improves. The readers left the study on the weakest rung. *"The results indicate that ILs are effective in avoiding mining-induced deforestation, preventing the accumulated deforestation of approximately 25 million hectares between 2004 and 2019."* IL is indigenous land.

Of the 43 journal studies: 12 at level 1, 9 at 2, 6 at 3 and 16 at 4. Of the 24 working papers: 2, 3, 7 and 12. In the exploratory search, 11 studies: 1, 2, none at 3, 7 at level 4 and 1 indeterminate. Studies that do not measure the episode's policy are in there. Level is not, by itself, that measure.

> **Methodological note.** Three layers, never added together. Peer-reviewed journals: other researchers read the text before it is published. That is 43 studies from the top quartile of SJR 2024, with a DOI (permanent identifier), Brazil in the title or abstract and a method word in the abstract, across 27 episodes. Working papers: texts from the Central Bank of Brazil, the BIS (Bank for International Settlements), the IMF (International Monetary Fund) or Ipea (Institute for Applied Economic Research), in OpenAlex (an open catalog), without journal review, at most 5 per episode. That is 24. Exploratory search: the same kind of text, with no institution filter, done afterwards, only for Pix (the instant-payment system), Central Bank autonomy and operating costs. That is 11.
>
> Two readers, Grok and Gemini, one study at a time, without seeing each other's output. Tiebreak written in advance: level goes to the larger number, the weaker one; "measures the episode", to the more conservative reading; a direction that does not match is marked discordant. Entering does not require measuring the policy. The titles were read before the rule closed, and that is stated in the amendment that records it.
>
> Kappa measures the agreement between the two readers beyond chance. The most fragile reading is whether the study measures the policy of the episode, and for it the more conservative value stands.
>
> On level, 43 is the number of studies. On "measures the episode", 47 counts each study-episode pair, because the same study can count for two. A 2008 profile of Alice Amsden entered under commodities and autonomy and measures neither. The word was *counterfactual* (what would have happened).

## In the journals, 7 of 27 episodes have a study that measures the policy; 4 without caveat; 10 with no study

The 7: Bolsa Família, commodities, Minha Casa Minha Vida, the 2014-16 recession, pensions, Auxílio Emergencial and deforestation. The 4 without caveat: Bolsa Família, Minha Casa Minha Vida, Auxílio Emergencial and deforestation. The 10 with zero: Real Plan, inflation targets, the Fiscal Responsibility Law, the 2008 crisis, subsidized BNDES (the national development bank) lending, tax breaks, Lava Jato, the spending cap, external debt and judicial independence. Another 10 got a study and none measures. Seven, ten and ten make 27. The 28th, cards and travel, has zero in the journals.

Zero in this filter is not zero in the literature. Left out are Ipea discussion papers, Brazilian journals and working papers.

## Measuring without caveat is not the same as finding improvement

Of the 43 journal studies, the final direction is 9 improves, 9 discordant, 3 mixed, 1 worsens and 21 not applicable. Not applicable: it entered and gives no signal about the policy. Of the 24 working papers: 15 not applicable, 6 discordant, 2 worsen and 1 improves. That improvement is a 2005 text on inflation expectations, level 4, with an empty abstract sentence. In the exploratory search, 11 studies: 5 not applicable, 2 improve, 1 worsens, 1 null, 1 mixed and 1 discordant. The lines do not add up.

Bolsa Família, journals: 6 entered, 4 measure without caveat, 2 in part. School, 2015, level 2, discordant. *"Weak aggregate effects mask considerable heterogeneity."* The same abstract records few significant impacts among boys. Of the 4 without caveat, the ones from 2020 and 2022 are protocols: they describe the calculation, and the direction is not applicable. Tuberculosis, 2018, level 2, improves. Food, 2016, in part, level 2, improves.

Deforestation: 8 entered, 1 without caveat, 4 in part. The one without caveat is from 2026, level 1, mixed. Rural credit and the 2004 and 2008 conservation policies are on rungs 1 and 2: in part, improves.

Minha Casa Minha Vida: 1 entered, without caveat, level 1, improves, municipalities from 2005 to 2019. *"Results indicate that treated municipalities experienced significant economic gains, with per capita GDP increasing by approximately 7.7% and formal wages rising by 5.6%."* GDP per capita is output divided by population.

Auxílio Emergencial: 1 entered, without caveat, level 1, mixed. *"Using data from an online survey, our results suggest that eligibility to the emergency cash transfer led to a reduced likelihood of individuals contracting COVID-19, likely to have been driven by a reduction in working hours."* The same abstract also records greater perceived severity and worsened misconceptions.

Lava Jato: no journal in this filter. Working paper from 2025, without caveat, level 1, worsens. *"Using a difference-in-differences analysis of unique, matched firm-bank data, we find sizable declines in employment and wage bills for investigated firms, alongside reduced access to credit."* The sentence is about the investigated firms. It is not this article's.

## A method word in the abstract gets a study in, and does not make it measure the policy

Minimum wage, since 2005: 4 journal studies, none measures. They got in through *calibrated* or *randomized*: dental trauma in children with cerebral palsy (2015), malocclusion in schoolchildren (2012) and the Pelotas birth cohorts of 2004 and 2015, published in 2010 and 2017. A cohort is a group followed from birth. Dentistry and cohorts are not the minimum wage.

On Pix, the journal that got in and does not measure is from 2025, on climate and supply chains. The words were *causal effects* and *difference-in-differences*. It is about neither fraud nor payment. The cap of 5, by OpenAlex relevance, caught noise, including a betting page, and left out a more fitting study. The exploratory search, with no institution filter, came afterwards.

## The spending cap goes in as a decision of Congress, even though the proposal came from the Executive

The decider is whoever held the power under the rule, not whoever had the idea. There are 124 rows, for the 28 episodes. Four of them were left without a verified basis: I did not find a primary rule to support the claim, and without a basis I do not assert it.

The 2016 cap is Congress alone, under Constitutional Amendment 95. The 2019 pension reform is also Congress alone. On inflation targets, the Central Bank row is without a verified basis; the Executive's has a basis, and Congress does not appear. On reserves and the IMF payoff, the Executive-and-Central-Bank row is without a verified basis. On the pandemic and press freedom, the Judiciary row too.

States and municipalities are in the rule of Bolsa Família, Lava Jato, the pandemic and deforestation. The world, as an outside shock, is in commodities, the 2008 crisis and the pandemic. This is power to decide, not effect.

## No journal study in the filter measures Pix or Central Bank autonomy

Before the search, the editor recorded: Pix and Central Bank autonomy are good policies. This article neither asserts nor tests the hypothesis.

In the journals, the two share one episode (2020-21), and autonomy has another: de facto since the 1999 targets and de jure through Complementary Law 179, of 2021. In both, there is a study that got in and none that measures. Working papers: zero.

What there is comes from the exploratory search, all in part. Pix, one text with no DOI in the record, level 1, improves: *"We find evidence that Pix increases the usage of the four most common payment technologies in Brazil among individuals and firms."* Another, from 2024, level 4, also improves.

Autonomy. Text from 2010, level 4, worsens, already in Portuguese: *"Assim, as relações do BCB com o Estado e a sociedade civil constituem uma deficiência do processo democrático brasileiro."* ("Thus, the BCB's relations with the State and civil society constitute a deficiency of the Brazilian democratic process.") Text from 2014, level 2, null direction. Text from 2006, level 4, mixed direction. None of these sentences was promoted to a conclusion here.

## The ratio by term runs from 0.0661% to 0.1474% of primary spending

The ratio describes cards and travel. It is not a grade for a term. The hypothesis that discipline in this spending matters was recorded and was not tested.

The numerator is CPGF (Federal Government Payment Card) card spending plus travel. Centralized purchasing and the Civil Defense card are excluded. In reais of September 2026, adjusted by the IPCA (the official inflation index). The ratio uses nominal values on both sides; the September 2026 reais serve to compare absolute values across years.

Dilma 2 and Temer are one term, 2015-2018, by the editor's decision. Dilma 1 has data only for 2014, 12 of 48 months. Lula 3 ends on December 31, 2026; the data run to July, 43 of 48 months, with no projection.

## No level of this ladder says who governed better

This measures what the literature has measured about policies. It does not measure competence and does not rank any government or head of government. States and municipalities are left for the next piece of work.

Back to the first dish: in the journals, 7 of the 27 episodes have some second dish, 4 of them without caveat, and 10 are left with no study in the filter.

Look at the dish without salt, the one in the first figure. No study is not no effect. A method word is not a cause. A cause, when there is one, is the study's sentence.
